## Why I built it

Job boards are noisy. Finding the few remote roles in Web3, payments and AI that actually fit my experience meant checking a dozen sites and reading past hundreds of listings that were never going to fit. I wanted that short list to arrive on its own, already filtered against my CV.

## How it works

The first version is deliberately low-tech. There’s no repository and no backend, just a folder of markdown files and scheduled tasks in Claude Cowork. One file holds my CV, another my criteria, another the companies worth watching, and a few prompts tell the agent how to search, score and summarise. On a schedule, a task reads the folder and writes the digest.

```
roleburn/
├── cv.md          # the source of truth
├── criteria.md    # remote from Europe, senior, Web3, payments, AI
├── companies.md   # teams worth watching
└── prompts/
    ├── search.md
    └── digest.md
```
