Search

Search result rows

Icon tile, title, its summary (shown in full: it wraps, so a match is never cut off), meta on the right; posts show their summary. The selected row is tinted indigo; ↵ shows what Enter does. Only the title and the summary are searched, each query word where a word starts, and every match is indigo at weight 600 with no background, so it stays readable on the tinted row. Topics aren’t searched (each has its own page); with no match, links to the topic pages are offered instead. Example: “agent”.

Used on: search menu (⌘K)
