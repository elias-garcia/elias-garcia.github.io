Lists and cards

List row

Date gutter of 112px, title, one muted line, and the whole row is the link. No dividers; other rows step back on hover. A row is a link only if it has somewhere to go; otherwise it is a plain row with no hover. For anything with a description, use an Entry.

Entry attachment

Optional media under an entry’s text, 16px below it, with 8px more under it before the next entry (an image has no space of its own above and below, as a line of text does, so this keeps entries evenly apart), starting exactly under the title (it wraps like the row: an empty 112px date column beside it, which drops away on phones together with the date). An entry has images or a link card, never both:

Photos: 1 to 3 images as 16:9 thumbnails, each a third of the text column, in a row of three (8px apart, 8px radius, hairline border); fewer stay left-aligned at the same size; never more than three. Every image has descriptive alt text. Each photo is a button (“Open photo 2 of 3: {entry title}”) that opens the image viewer. On narrow rows (phones, where the date sits above the title) the photos show full width in the page instead: one fills the text column; several scroll sideways one at a time, each as wide as a single photo, with a 2px line 12px under the strip: its marker is as long as one photo’s share of the set and moves as you swipe, so it shows there are more and where you are. It switches at the row’s own width (436px, the point where the date moves up), not a screen breakpoint, so a tablet with the date column keeps the thumbnails. Video preview: exactly one bordered card spanning the full text column (the same width as a row of photos), with a thumbnail the same size and shape as a photo (a third of the column, 16:9) and a 32px play button centred on it, a label and its source with ↗, e.g. “Watch the trailer / YouTube ↗”. It is its own link, separate from the row’s link; it opens the video elsewhere and never embeds a player.

Link: one card linking to a page, built from that page’s share data. See Link attachment below.

Link attachment

One bordered card, a single link to another page, in a row’s attachment slot. The entry gives only the address; the title, image and site name come from that page’s share data (Open Graph, or its <title>), fetched at build time, and any of them can be overridden. Image in the first third (16:9, the shape of video thumbnails and close to share images; cropped to fill), then the title (14/500, two lines at most) and the site line (14px --text-3: site name · domain, one line, then ↗). A video link gets the play button. Hover and focus: border --line-strong, title --accent; press .99.

Worst cases: no image keeps the frame with a link icon, so every card has the same shape; no title shows the address; a failed fetch keeps the last saved copy, or falls back to address and icon. The build warns and never fails.

Used on: Home (Teaching). Any row can carry one: work rows (last in the open body), talks, courses, education, posts.

Image viewer

Opens when a photo attachment is clicked. A modal dialog in the search palette’s language: a card up to 840px wide (70% of the window, never taller than it) with the photo on top, cropped to fill a 16:9 frame so the card is the same size for every photo, the talk title, place and date below with the position (“1 / 3”), and a footer of key hints; ‹ › on the photo’s sides, × in its corner, all 40px round buttons. The page behind dims with the scrim and doesn’t scroll. Focus moves to the viewer itself on open (no ring on it; Tab reaches × and ‹ ›), stays inside while open and returns to the thumbnail on close. Esc, ×, the backdrop and a swipe down close it; ← → and swipes move. One photo: no arrows and no count. Phones (under 640px): the platform photo viewer, always on black: photo edge to edge, caption below it, then ‹ › with dots at the bottom within thumb reach; × top right; 44px buttons. Dragging down moves the photo and closes it past 80px, otherwise it springs back. Reduced motion: a fade only.

Used on: Home (talk photos). To see it open over the page, set Tweaks › Image viewer › open on any Home artboard.

Entry

Every Home section is a list of entries: the date column (one date, or start – end), the title, the grey line, then the description, open, then up to three images or one link card. The title is the link when the entry has one (an underline fades in on hover; ↗ when it leaves the site), not the whole row, because a description can hold its own links. The description is Markdown: 16px --text-2 at 1.6, 8px below the grey line, paragraphs and lists 8px apart, links inside it are prose links. Work adds its place after the title (“Vesu · Remote”) and is collapsible (next).

Entry, collapsible

An Entry with collapsible on: the head (dates, title, place, the grey line) is the button and the description and attachment open under it, 8px below the grey line as in any entry. Every job starts closed and each opens on its own; + turns into × when open. A job with nothing to open is a plain row: no +, not a button. Hovering a job fades the other jobs, as post rows do, and the + darkens. The head is a button, so the title is never a link here. The company line carries the work mode in the role line’s grey 14px after a “·”: “Remote”, or the mode and city (“On-site, A Coruña”, “Hybrid, A Coruña”); plain text, no chips or icons. The first line is top-aligned: the date, the company and the 16px +/× share one 24px line, the icon centred in a 24px box, on desktop and on phones (where the date and icon share the first line).

Prose

Heading, paragraph, link, footnote

Every other Markdown element (lists, quotes, tables, inline code, checklists, callouts) is on the Prose reference artboard.

Section headings get a # anchor on hover. Links inside prose are indigo with a soft underline and never carry the ↗ arrow; the arrow is only for standalone links (contact rows, entry titles that leave the site). Footnote markers are links to a Notes section at the end of the article, below a short 64px rule (numbered, 14px grey). Each note ends with a ↩ link (24px target) back to its marker, which lands a third of the way down the screen. Arriving at a note or back at a marker briefly lights it up. No popovers. Rhythm: 16px between blocks; 48px above an h2 and 16px below it, 32px above an h3 and 12px below, 24px above an h4 and 8px below; 24px around figures, code blocks and tables; 8px between list items.

Used on: posts
