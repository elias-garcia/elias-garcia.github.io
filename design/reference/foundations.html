Typography

One family, Instrument Sans, in three weights: 400 regular, 500 medium, 600 semibold. The base is 16px (1rem), and it is the size of everything you read or navigate with, on every page: paragraphs, titles, the lines under them, dates and the header menu. Hierarchy comes from colour and weight first, as on read.cv; size steps in only for page titles (20) and inside posts (32, 24, 20, a 1.25 scale from the base). 14 is for facts and compact controls, 12 for the smallest marks. Six sizes: 32, 24, 20, 16, 14, 12, set in rem. Line heights: 1.2 display, 1.35 headings and titles, 1.6 body, 1.5 single lines and small text, 1.7 code.

Spacing

Ten steps on Tailwind’s 4px grid, each with a job (the right-hand number is the Tailwind step: p-4 = 16px). Space follows proximity: a heading sits closer to its own content than to what came before it, and the gap grows with the size of the break. Section headings are the size of the text, so the space above them does the separating. 20px is only for padding inside boxed components (code lines, captions, demos), never between blocks. Only 1–2px hairlines sit off the grid.

Motion

Three durations and two curves; interface feedback never takes longer than 250ms. Hover movement only runs on devices with a real pointer. With reduced motion on, pages fade in without moving and everything else is instant. Three things sit outside the scale on purpose: the Roleburn preview morphing into its case study between pages (400ms), the easing demo in the blog post (600ms), and the slow loops inside project previews.
