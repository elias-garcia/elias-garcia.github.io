The building blocks every component uses: colour, type and spacing. Colours are Tailwind zinc and indigo only: 14 tokens, each with a light and a dark value, switched by the visitor’s system setting (prefers-color-scheme). Components use these tokens, never raw values.

Typography

One family, Instrument Sans, in three weights: 400 regular, 500 medium, 600 semibold. Eight sizes: 28, 20, 18, 16, 15, 14, 13, 12. Five line heights: 1 for single-line controls, 1.2 display, 1.35 headings and compact rows, 1.6 interface text, 1.75 long-form and code. Headings tighten their letter spacing slightly; everything else stays at 0.

Motion

Three durations and two curves; interface feedback never takes longer than 250ms. Hover movement only runs on devices with a real pointer. With reduced motion on, pages fade in without moving and everything else is instant. Three things sit outside the scale on purpose: the Roleburn preview morphing into its case study between pages (400ms), the easing demo in the blog post (600ms), and the slow loops inside project previews.
